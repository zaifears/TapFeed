Recordings got a big upgrade: they start playing almost at once, show what the camera detected, and come with thumbnails.

## Download

**`TapFeed.exe`** below. Double-click it; nothing to install. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. If you have 2.0.0, just replace the file.

Windows SmartScreen may say it protects your PC because the app is new and not code-signed. Select **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **Watch without waiting**: a recording starts playing a second or two after you click it, while the rest arrives. Recordings you watch are kept in a cache (up to 1 GB, oldest out first) and open instantly next time; Settings shows its size and can clear it.
- **What the camera saw**: each recording is labelled with its detections (Person, Vehicle, Pet, Motion and more), with a filter to show just one kind.
- **Thumbnails** from the camera on every detection recording, newest first, with a button to flip the order.
- **Loading you can see**: a spinner and percentage while a recording loads, and "Buffering…" if playback catches up with the download.
- **Remembers the window**: size, position and monitor are restored when you open TapFeed.
- **Saved passwords look saved**: the password box shows dots when a password is saved on this PC, and Enter signs straight in.

## Fixed

- Downloads could sit at 0% or appear to restart, and Cancel could be ignored; downloads now start promptly and Cancel stops them within a second.
- Downloading a recording you had just watched could stay at 0% forever.
- A recording cut short by the camera could be saved as if complete; it is now reported as failed.
- Live view pauses while a recording downloads (the camera can only send one at a time) and resumes afterwards.
