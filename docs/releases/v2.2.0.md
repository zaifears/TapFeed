Two-way audio arrives on supported cameras, recording thumbnails load progressively instead of waiting on whole batches, and saved pan/tilt positions work reliably.

## Download

**`TapFeed.exe`** below. Double-click it; nothing to install. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. If you have 2.1.0, just replace the file.

Windows SmartScreen may say it protects your PC because the app is new and not code-signed. Select **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **Two-way audio (Talk)**: checked automatically at sign-in; cameras with a speaker show a **Talk** button beside the volume slider and a **Mic live** indicator while speaking. Choose your input device under **Settings → Microphone** (with **Refresh microphones** to list all Windows mics).
- **Much faster recording thumbnails**: thumbnails now stop waiting as soon as each JPEG arrives and stream into the list one by one instead of waiting on an entire batch.
- **Updated camera engine**: upgraded to **pytapo 3.4.34** (off-loop AES decryption during downloads, cleaner cancellation socket cleanup, and native local two-way audio).

## Fixed

- Selecting a **Saved position** could fail with *"The camera did not move. It may be at the end of its range."* because the preset ID was overwritten by the internal helper request ID.
- Missing or deleted saved positions now report a clear message instead of claiming the motor reached its limit.
